Dr. Jing-Mei Li

Dr. Jing-Mei Li is currently a Postdoc Researcher and Software Engineer at the Institute for Genomic Statistics and Bioinformatics, University Hospital Bonn. She received her Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from National Tsing Hua University, Taiwan

Her current work involves developing deep learning platforms for genetic clinicians, specifically the GestaltMatcher Database.

Research and Working Experience

Dr. Ting-Chun Lin

Dr. Ting-Chun Lin is a doctoral student (Dr. med.) at the University Bonn. With a clinical background in general medicine, she is currently exploring patient perspectives on the use of AI-based diagnostic tools, especially facial analysis systems, in routine clinical care.

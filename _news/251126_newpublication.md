New Publication in Nature Communications: Delineating PSMC5-related Disorders

---
We are proud to announce our participation in the UPS-NDDiag consortium. Our team contributed to the delineation of facial phenotypes in individuals with PSMC5-related disorders.

This collaborative effort, titled "Investigating the neuronal role of the proteasomal ATPase subunit gene PSMC5 in neurodevelopmental proteasomopathies," has been published in Nature Communications. We extend our gratitude to Sébastien KÜRY and all co-authors for this impactful collaboration.
